## 2. Look-Alike / Confusing Terms

### Group A: The 4 Local IT Service Markets
| Term | What it is | Key difference | Clue words in a question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IT Consulting and Implementation** | Advisory services to evaluate and align tech strategies | Focuses on **strategy, advice, and alignment**, not daily operations | "advisory", "evaluate technology strategies", "align with business", "cloud strategy", "intelligent operating models" |
| **Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)** | Transfer of an **entire business process** to an IT-based third party | Transfers a whole business function (HR, call centers), not just IT tasks | "entire business process", "back office", "front office", "human resources", "call centers", "voice-related" |
| **IT Outsourcing (ITO)** | External contracting of **IT functions, services, and projects** | Contracts specific **IT** work (devs, network, infra) rather than whole non-IT business processes | "IT functions", "software developers", "network management", "infrastructure maintenance", "save office space/utilities" |
| **Other IT Services** | Catch-all for remaining IT services | Covers **integration, installation/support, repairs, and training** | "not specifically mentioned", "system integration", "software installation", "education and training", "screen/battery repairs" |

**Trap:** BPO transfers an *entire business process* (like HR or call centers), whereas ITO contracts *IT-specific functions/projects* (like software developers or network management).

### Group B: BPO Process Locations
| Term | What it is | Key difference | Clue words in a question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Back Office** | Internal BPO business processes | Does not interact directly with external customers | "human resources", internal operations |
| **Front Office** | Customer-facing BPO business processes | Direct interaction with customers | "customer service", "call centers", "voice-related" |

**Trap:** Both are BPO processes—do not confuse "Front Office" with "Other IT Services" (e.g., mall repair stalls or social media support).

### Group C: Philippine Government & Industry Bodies/Programs
| Term | What it is | Key difference | Clue words in a question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IBPAP** | Industry association (IT and Business Process Association of the Philippines) | Private/industry association that tracks/estimates market stats ($29B, 1.44M workers) | "estimated $29 billion", "120,000 full-time employees", "1.44 million workforce" |
| **DICT** | Government department (Department of Information and Communications Technology) | Government agency that launched 'Digital Cities 2025' | "launched 'Digital Cities 2025'", "25 areas outside Metro Manila" |
| **Special Economic Zone Act of 1995** | Philippine legislation/law | Law that established **ecozones and ICT hubs** | "1995", "ecozones", "ICT hubs", "implemented by the Philippines government" |

**Trap:** IBPAP is the *association* reporting the 2021 figures ($29B / 1.44M), while DICT is the *government department* behind Digital Cities 2025, and the 1995 Act is the *law* creating ecozones/ICT hubs.

### Group D: Enterprise Segments in Statista Data
| Term | What it is | Key difference | Clue words in a question |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **B2G** | Business-to-Government | Client is a government entity | "government", "B2G" |
| **B2B** | Business-to-Business | Client is another business | "enterprises", "other businesses", "B2B" |
| **B2C** | Business-to-Customer | Client is an individual consumer | "individual customers", "consumers", "B2C" |

**Trap:** Statista's global IT service figures include *all three* (B2G, B2B, B2C)—watch out for "Which is NOT" questions that sneak in a fake acronym like B2E or G2C.

## 3. Likely Distractors

| Major Term | 3 Most Likely Distractors | How to Tell Them Apart in One Line |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **IT Consulting and Implementation** | BPO, ITO, Other IT Services | Consulting *advises and aligns* tech strategy; BPO runs whole processes, ITO contracts IT tasks, Other covers integration/install/training. |
| **Business Process Outsourcing (BPO)** | ITO, IT Consulting, Other IT Services | BPO transfers an *entire business process* (HR/call centers); ITO contracts *IT functions/projects*; Consulting *advises*; Other covers *training/repairs*. |
| **IT Outsourcing (ITO)** | BPO, IT Consulting, Other IT Services | ITO contracts *IT functions/projects* (devs, network); BPO transfers *entire business processes*; Consulting *advises*; Other covers *installation/training*. |
| **Other IT Services** | IT Consulting, ITO, BPO | Only *Other IT Services* covers system integration, software installation/support, repairs, and IT education/training. |
| **Back Office (BPO)** | Front Office, ITO, Other IT Services | Back Office = *internal* processes like HR; Front Office = *call centers*; ITO = *IT functions*; Other = *repairs/training*. |
| **Front Office (BPO)** | Back Office, ITO, Other IT Services | Front Office = *customer service in call centers*; Back Office = *HR*; ITO = *network/software dev*; Other = *certification training*. |
| **IBPAP** | DICT, Special Economic Zone Act of 1995, CNCTC, Inc. | IBPAP is the *association* estimating $29B/1.44M; DICT runs *Digital Cities 2025*; 1995 Act creates *ecozones*; CNCTC does *training*. |
| **Digital Cities 2025** | Special Economic Zone Act of 1995, IBPAP, Digital Adoption | Digital Cities 2025 is DICT's *25-area program outside Metro Manila*; 1995 Act = *ecozones*; IBPAP = *association*; Digital Adoption = *pandemic shift*. |
| **Automation** | Digital Adoption, Tailored Pricing, Cloud Strategy | Automation specifically *eliminates monotonous tasks*; Digital Adoption is the *pandemic shift* (remote work/security); Cloud Strategy is *Accenture's consulting*. |
| **China (Global Player)** | Japan, United Kingdom, Germany | China is *4th in 2023 ($73B)*, has the *highest growth rate (8.58%)* among the top 5, *outpaces Japan by $4B in 2028 ($110B)*, and is the only top-5 player where *BPO beats Other IT Services*. |

## Answer Key

1. **B** — IT Consulting and Implementation covers advisory services to evaluate technology strategies and align them with business strategies or internal processes. *Why A/C are wrong:* BPO transfers an entire process; ITO contracts specific IT tasks/projects.
2. **C** — BPO transfers an entire business process, and human resources is specifically cited as a back-office process. *Why B is wrong:* Front office refers to customer-facing processes like call centers.
3. **C** — ITO is the external contracting of IT functions, services, and projects (like software dev and network management) to focus on main functions and save office space, maintenance, and utilities. *Why A is wrong:* BPO transfers entire non-IT business processes.
4. **D** — Other IT Services covers system integration, software installation and support, and IT education and training. *Why A is wrong:* Consulting focuses on technology strategy alignment, not installation or training.
5. **B** — Accenture offers IT consulting and implementation (cloud strategy and design; intelligent operating models) outside of its BPO operations. *Why A is wrong:* The handout explicitly notes Accenture offers these *outside* of BPO.
6. **C** — Cloud Infrastructure Manufacturing is not one of the four markets; the four are IT Consulting & Implementation, BPO, ITO, and Other IT Services. *Why D is wrong:* Other IT Services is one of the four official markets.
7. **B** — DICT launched the 'Digital Cities 2025' program to assist 25 areas outside Metro Manila in becoming digital cities by 2025. *Why A is wrong:* The 1995 Act established ecozones and ICT hubs.
8. **B** — The Special Economic Zone Act of 1995 was implemented by the Philippine government to establish ecozones and ICT hubs. *Why D is wrong:* IBPAP is an industry association, not a law.
9. **C** — Facebook employs software specialists and subcontracts customer support providers and content moderators in the Philippines. *Why D is wrong:* HP works with offshoring providers for customer support and IT services, not content moderators.
10. **C** — Microsourcing is an IT Outsourcing (ITO) company, not an IT Consulting and Implementation company. *Why A/B/D are wrong:* Emerio, Tsukiden, and Global BrainForce are all listed under IT Consulting and Implementation.
11. **C** — Yondu is listed under IT Outsourcing (ITO), not BPO. *Why A/B/D are wrong:* Alorica, Concentrix, and Teleperformance are all listed as popular BPO companies.
12. **B** — Computer Networking Career & Training Center, Inc. (CNCTC, Inc.) offers certification training in RedHat, Microsoft, TESDA, Azure, and Amazon AWS. *Why A/C/D are wrong:* Global BrainForce and Genie Technologies are consulting firms; Sourcefit is an ITO firm.
13. **B** — The handout states social media marketing is an easier way to reach potential customers than mall stalls where providers call passersby regardless of intent. *Why A is wrong:* Ecozones apply to the IT-BPO industry under the 1995 Act.
14. **B** — The Philippines' IT-BPO sector makes up 13% of global market share and ranks 1st in voice-related services (Outsourcing Journal). *Why A is wrong:* 29 ($29B) was the 2021 revenue estimated by IBPAP, not global market share.
15. **B** — The handout states companies implement IT services due to the advent of automation that eliminates monotonous tasks. *Why A is wrong:* Tailored pricing is a change in client demand, not automation.
16. **B** — Due to the COVID-19 pandemic, companies realized the necessity for digital adoption (remote work support, software support, data security). *Why D is wrong:* System integration is a category under Other IT Services.
17. **C** — IT Outsourcing (ITO) is the dominant market in the US ($168B), UK ($32B), Japan ($33B), China ($26B), and Germany ($25B). *Why A is wrong:* BPO is 2nd in China and 3rd in the other four countries.
18. **C** — China ($73B in 2023) is projected to reach $110B by 2028 at an 8.58% annual growth rate, outpacing Japan ($106B) by $4B. *Why A is wrong:* The UK grows at 6.26% to $117B and is already ahead of Japan in 2023.
19. **D** — In China, BPO is 2nd ($23.13B) and Other IT Services is 3rd ($19.75B). *Why A/B/C are wrong:* In the US, UK, Japan (and Germany), Other IT Services is 2nd and BPO is 3rd.
20. **C** — Business-to-Employee (B2E) is not mentioned; Statista's data includes B2G, B2B, and B2C. *Why A/B/D are wrong:* All three are explicitly listed in the handout.
21. **C** — The market is updated twice a year in case market dynamics change (such as a pandemic or war). *Why B is wrong:* Twice a year (biannually), not annually.
22. **B** — This is the handout's definition of an **economy** ("Simply, an economy is the structure of production, distribution, and utilization..."). *Why A is wrong:* The IT Services Market is defined as covering services used to create, manage, and deliver information.
